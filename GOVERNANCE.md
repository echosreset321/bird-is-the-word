# Interim governance
Status: maintainer-led seed, open to revision. Repository owner echosreset321 is the initial steward; no elected council or decentralized governance exists.

Small fixes may be merged by the steward after review and passing tests. Changes to the contract, charter, data handling, funding, or governance require a public issue describing purpose, alternatives, affected people, and risks, open for at least seven calendar days before decision. The steward records reasons and dissent in the issue. Silence is not consent to data use.

Contributors may propose maintainers by issue. Appointment requires their explicit agreement, a record of contributions, and a public steward decision. Future shared governance should specify decision rights and a transition process before claiming to exist. Disclose conflicts of interest and recuse from decisions about personal financial benefit.

Disputes: describe behavior and desired repair without private details; steward acknowledges, hears affected contributors, and records a reasoned decision. Appeal through a new issue requesting independent review; if no independent reviewer is available, state that limit. Harassment, impersonation, doxxing, coercion, and discriminatory abuse may lead to moderation or removal. Urgent harm can justify immediate moderation, with a sanitized explanation afterward.

No exclusive contributor agreement or transfer of copyright is required. Anyone may fork under MIT. Project membership never grants access to another person's data.
