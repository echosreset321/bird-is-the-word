# Roadmap
## Implemented
Local v0.1 contract validation, synthetic example and tests; seed governance and invitation.

## Proposed / help wanted
1. Consent UX: accessible local preview and explicit per-transfer approval, including withdrawal limitations.
2. Interoperability pilot: one hosted and one local adapter; synthetic end-to-end demonstration with no automatic execution.
3. Threat model: sender impersonation, prompt injection, secret leakage, recipient retention, and replay.
4. Community stewardship: invite independent maintainers; discuss benefit retention, transparent costs, and decision rights.
5. Companion portability: explore user-controlled context exports without promises of model identity or continuity.

Success means a person can understand and choose a small transfer between independently operated tools. No rollout date, adoption count, or funding commitment is assumed.
